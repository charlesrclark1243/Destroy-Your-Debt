Split this month's payment

Enter how much you can pay each month, add any months that will be different, and pick a strategy. Every strategy pays all minimums first, keeps paid-off cards clear of new charges, and sets aside enough to clear deferred-interest promotions before they end; they differ in where the rest goes. The projection follows your amounts, new charges and promotional rates until you're debt-free.
